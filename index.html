Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

manyin-sausin (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the mobile application 文言修仙傳 (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

Account Information
When you sign in using Google or Apple Sign-In, we receive your name and email address as provided by the authentication provider. We do not collect or store your password. You may also use Guest Mode, which creates an anonymous account identifier without any name or email address; guest progress cannot be recovered after signing out or deleting the App.

Subscription & Payment Information
If you subscribe to premium features, payment is processed entirely by Apple (In-App Purchase, on iPhone and iPad) or Google Play (Google Play Billing, on Android). We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card number, bank account, or other financial details. We receive only a transaction confirmation and subscription status from Apple or Google Play.

Leaderboard Profile
When you choose a cultivation name (仙名) and character avatar, we store them together with your cultivation points (修為) and level on our servers (Google Cloud Firestore). This information is shown publicly to other players on the in-app leaderboard (青雲榜). Please do not use your real name or other personal information as your cultivation name.

Messages with Friends (千里傳音)
If you connect with a friend using their friend code (道號), the messages and quick phrases you exchange are stored on our servers (Google Cloud Firestore) so they can be delivered and so that reported messages can be reviewed. We keep only the most recent 200 messages for each pair of friends. Messages are deleted when either friend ends the friendship, blocks the other, or deletes their account. Guest users can only send preset quick phrases. Reports you file (the reported name or message and the reason) are kept so we can enforce our community rules.

Learning Progress
Your quiz answers, progress, wrong-answer review list, items and settings are stored on your device. Your cultivation points, level and chosen companion pet are sent to our servers for the leaderboard.

Account Activity Record
Each time you open the App or return to it, we store the date and time of your latest activity, the date this record was first created, and the App version you are using on our servers (Google Cloud Firestore). This record is linked to your account identifier, including for Guest Mode accounts. It is kept until your account is deleted.

App Analytics
We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used. It records events such as opening the App, starting or completing a level (including the level number and text title), viewing the subscription page and completing a subscription (including the product and price). It also collects an app-instance identifier, general technical information such as device model, operating system and App version, and an approximate location derived from a masked IP address. We do not send your name, email address, cultivation name, messages or account identifier to Google Analytics, and we do not collect your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone and iPad, Advertising ID on Android). Analytics data is not removed when you delete your account because it is not linked to your account; it is kept according to our Google Analytics retention setting of up to 14 months.

Anonymous Usage Statistics
Separately from App Analytics, and to understand how the free and subscription versions are used, the App counts a few events per day, for example that a device opened the App, reached the daily free limit, viewed the subscription page or completed a subscription. Each event is counted at most once per device per day and is added to an anonymous daily total on our servers. These totals contain no account identifier, name, email address, device identifier or advertising identifier, and cannot be linked to you.

No Advertising or Tracking
The App does not show advertising, does not use advertising services, does not sell your personal information, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide and maintain the App’s features and services
  • To process and manage your subscription
  • To show your cultivation name, avatar and level on the public leaderboard
  • To deliver messages between friends and to review reported content
  • To understand how the App and its free and subscription features are used, and to improve them
  • To know when accounts were last active and which App versions are in use
  • To communicate with you regarding support or service-related matters

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — to manage user sign-in. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Cloud Firestore (Google) — to store leaderboard profiles, friend messages, account activity records and anonymous usage totals. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (Google) — to understand how the App is used. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Apple In-App Purchase — to process subscription payments on iPhone and iPad. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — to process subscription payments on Android. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — to provide authentication. Google Privacy Policy
  • Sign in with Apple — to provide authentication. Apple Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion of your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it.

5. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by:

  • Using the “Delete Account” (刪除帳號) option in the App’s menu
  • Contacting us at manyinsausin@gmail.com

Deleting your account in the App immediately removes your sign-in account, account activity record, leaderboard profile, friend code, friendships and messages. Upon receiving a request by email, we will delete your personal data within 30 days. Anonymous usage totals and App Analytics data are not linked to your account and are not affected. Cancelling a subscription is done separately in your Apple ID or Google Play settings.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information using industry-standard security practices, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS) and secure authentication protocols. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at manyinsausin@gmail.com.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.
