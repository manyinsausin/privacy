1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

When you sign in using Google or Apple Sign-In, we receive your name and email address as provided by the authentication provider. We do not collect or store your password. You may also use Guest Mode, which creates an anonymous account identifier without any name or email address; guest progress cannot be recovered after signing out or deleting the App.

If you subscribe to premium features, payment is processed entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card number, bank account, or other financial details. We receive only a transaction confirmation and subscription status from Apple.

Your quiz answers, progress, wrong-answer review list and settings are stored on your device. Only your cultivation points and level are sent to our servers for the leaderboard.

No Analytics or Advertising
The App does not use analytics or advertising services and does not track you across other apps or websites.
